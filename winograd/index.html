How to Play

Each puzzle shows a sentence with one word highlighted — usually a pronoun like "it" or "they". That word could refer to more than one thing in the sentence, and your job is to work out which one makes sense.

Read the whole sentence carefully. The answer usually depends on the meaning of the sentence, not just the grammar — the same pronoun can point to a different word if a single detail in the sentence changes.

Pick the option you think the highlighted word refers to, then click "Check" to see if you're right.

Start mode moves through easy, medium, and hard sentences in sequence. Infinite mode lets you pick the difficulty yourself.
