How to Play

This puzzle shows Bongard-style examples split into a left class and a right class. Each side follows a hidden rule.


Study the examples and look for the shared pattern on each side. The answer is a sentence that describes the left class and the right class.


Use the dropdowns to complete the sentence at the bottom. Then press Check.


Start mode moves through easy, medium, difficult, and extra hard problems in sequence. Infinite mode lets you pick the difficulty yourself.
